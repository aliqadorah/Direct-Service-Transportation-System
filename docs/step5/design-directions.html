Design directions — step 5

Three directions for Direct Service Transportation within the fixed brand: the colours, typefaces, light theme and right-to-left Arabic layout are the same in all three. What changes is the layout, the navigation, the density and the component style. Each direction shows the same five screens with real transport content: sign-in, dashboard, the trips grid with quick entry, the trip form and a driver profile. Pick one (or say which parts to combine); full mockups of every screen follow in step 6.
