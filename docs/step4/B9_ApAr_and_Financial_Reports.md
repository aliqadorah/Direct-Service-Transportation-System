## Code review — summary (independent reviewer + author)
No High findings. Entry balancing (documents, payments, returns with the exact split and the 0.010 absorption), voids (period on the document date, reversal re-checked under the journal lock, pending postings closed in module ACC), gap-free numbers, permissions, report sign conventions and roll-ups, and the unchanged behaviour of the e-invoicing refactor for invoices and credit notes were checked and found sound.
- **Fixed:**
  - M1: a party's control account could change after documents were posted, so later payments/returns would post to another account than the document; the control account (and role) is now fixed once documents or journal lines use the party (L4 included journal lines in the role guard).
  - M2: the reconciliation compared the ledger only with party sums of the same lines; each control account now also shows the sub-ledger rebuilt from the documents as of the date (AP/AR documents of its parties, transport invoices less on-account receipts on trade receivables, partner payable, unsettled driver advances) and its difference; driver payable is not rebuilt (it depends on the accrual setting at each trip's save) and stays line-based (DT-104).
  - L1 unmatched-line filter by (type, id) pairs; L2 first receivables of the same transport customer serialised under the ARD- number lock before the accounting party is created; L3 payment date not before the document date; L5 comment; L6 the opening entry is no longer removed by the delete actions (opening balances are cleared only by a full reset); L7 exports read the ledger in one ordered query; L8 the backup is a POST (it writes the maintenance log).
